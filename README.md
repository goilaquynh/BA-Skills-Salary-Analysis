# BA-Skills-Salary-Analysis
# Breaking Into Business Analyst — Skills & Salary Analysis

## Project Overview
As a Business Analytics student curious about my own career path, I analyzed 271 US BA job postings to understand what skills employers actually want and what salaries look like across experience levels.

## Objective
Identify the most in-demand technical skills and salary trends for Business Analyst roles in the US market to guide entry-level candidates in prioritizing their skill development.

## Key Findings
- SQL is the most consistent skill across all experience levels
- Python demand stays strong from Entry to Senior roles
- Average salaries: Entry $89K → Mid $113K → Senior $139K (55% growth)
- Technical skills are the baseline — communication is the differentiator

## Tools Used
- Excel (data cleaning & analysis)
- Canva (visualization)
- Kaggle — AI Job Market Dataset (2024)

## Dataset
Source: Kaggle — AI Job Market Dataset (2024)
Sample size: 271 US BA job postings
